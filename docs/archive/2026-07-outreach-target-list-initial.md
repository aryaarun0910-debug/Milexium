# Outreach target list — paratransit / NEMT (US)

Priority order: smaller regional agencies and one association contact first (highest reply-rate), Medicaid NEMT brokers second, large metro agencies last (slowest, least likely to reply to a student email).

## Tier 1 — highest-leverage single action: CTAA

**Community Transportation Association of America** (ctaa.org) — national association for 1,200+ rural/small-urban/specialized community transportation providers. This is exactly your target customer profile in one place.
- Action: email CTAA directly (not a member agency) explaining the research angle and asking if they can connect you with 2-3 member agencies who'd be open to a call, or point you to their annual conference/membership directory.
- Why first: one email, potential access to dozens of qualified leads via a warm intro instead of 20 cold ones.
