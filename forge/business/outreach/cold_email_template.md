# Paratransit cold outreach — email template

Target: ops managers / scheduling leads at small-to-mid US paratransit operators and NEMT brokers. Not the giants (Uber Health, big city DOT contracts) first — smaller regional operators are more likely to reply and talk candidly about what breaks.

Ask is a research call, not a sales pitch. Keep it under 100 words.

---

Subject: Quick question about your dispatch/scheduling — 15 min?

Hi [Name],

I'm Arya, an engineering student at Loughborough University in the UK. My teammate Chris and I built a predictive demand + routing system for underserved bus service in Birmingham — it beat 13,000+ students to reach the Grand Final of the Engineers Without Borders design challenge (real transit data, 0.94 R² demand model, live dashboard).

We're now looking at whether the same approach — predicting demand and explaining routing decisions, rather than just optimizing a black box — could help US paratransit operators. Before we build anything specific, we want to learn where scheduling actually breaks down day-to-day for you.

Would you have 15 minutes this week or next for a call? Happy to work around your schedule.

Thanks,
Arya
